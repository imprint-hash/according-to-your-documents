# Kestrel Haulage & Fulfilment — Internal Knowledge Base (v4.2)

*Fictional company created for this study. Every fact below is invented.*

## 1. About us
Kestrel Haulage & Fulfilment ("Kestrel") is a regional freight and warehousing company founded in 2014. Head office: Unit 9, Larkspur Trade Park. We operate 3 warehouses: **Larkspur (LK1)**, **Millbrook (MB2)** and **Ashgate (AG3)**. Customer support runs Monday–Friday, 07:30–18:00, and Saturday 09:00–13:00. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## 2. Service tiers
| Tier | Delivery promise | Price per parcel (up to 5 kg) | Extra per kg above 5 kg |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Saver** | 4–6 working days | 6.40 | 0.85 |
| **Standard** | 2–3 working days | 9.10 | 1.20 |
| **Express** | Next working day if booked before 14:00 | 17.75 | 2.05 |

All prices are in **Kestrel credits (KC)**, our billing unit (1 KC is invoiced at the customer's contracted rate). Parcels over **30 kg** are not accepted on any parcel tier; they must be booked as **Pallet Freight** (section 3).

## 3. Pallet Freight
- Standard pallet (up to 600 kg): **142 KC** per pallet.
- Half pallet (up to 300 kg): **88 KC**.
- Tail-lift delivery surcharge: **19 KC** per drop.
- Pallets must be booked at least **2 working days** in advance through the customer portal.

## 4. Refunds and claims
- Late delivery refund: if an **Express** parcel arrives late, the customer receives a **full refund of the Express price**. **Standard** late deliveries receive a **40% refund**. **Saver** deliveries have **no late-delivery refund**.
- Damage claims must be filed within **7 calendar days** of delivery, with photos. Claims are reviewed within **10 working days**.
- Maximum compensation for a lost or damaged parcel is **250 KC** unless the customer bought **Kestrel Cover**.
- **Kestrel Cover** costs **3% of the declared value** and raises the compensation limit to the declared value, up to **5,000 KC**.

## 5. Prohibited items
We do not carry: lithium batteries shipped on their own (batteries installed inside a device are allowed), aerosols, liquids over 1 litre per bottle, live animals, cash, or firearms. Perfume is allowed only in its original sealed packaging, up to 250 ml per parcel.

## 6. Account and payment terms
- New business accounts get **14-day payment terms**. After 6 months with no late payments, terms extend to **30 days**.
- Late payment fee: **2.5%** of the overdue amount per month.
- Minimum monthly spend for a business account: **500 KC**. Accounts below this for **3 consecutive months** move to pay-as-you-go.
- Volume discount: **8%** off parcel prices for accounts shipping more than **1,000 parcels per month**.

## 7. Collections
- Free scheduled collection for accounts shipping **50 or more parcels per week**.
- Ad-hoc collection: **12 KC** per collection, book before **11:00** for same-day pickup.
- Collection windows: morning (08:00–12:00) or afternoon (13:00–17:00). We cannot guarantee a specific hour.

## 8. Tracking and delivery attempts
- Tracking updates are sent by email and SMS. Tracking numbers start with **KHF-** followed by 10 digits.
- We make **2 delivery attempts**. After the second failed attempt the parcel is held at the nearest warehouse for **5 working days**, then returned to sender at the sender's cost (**4.50 KC** return fee).
- Safe-place delivery is available only if the recipient opts in through the tracking page.

## 9. Warehousing (fulfilment customers)
- Storage: **1.10 KC per pallet space per day**.
- Pick-and-pack: **0.95 KC per order** plus **0.20 KC per extra item** in the same order.
- Stock counts: one free full stock count per quarter; extra counts cost **75 KC**.
- Goods-in: deliveries to our warehouses must be pre-advised **24 hours** in advance; unannounced deliveries may be refused.

## 10. Data and privacy
Customer data is kept for **6 years** after the account closes, for tax purposes. Recipient phone numbers are used only for delivery updates. Kestrel does not sell customer data.

## 11. Escalations
First contact: support desk (portal chat or email). If unresolved after **5 working days**, a case can be escalated to the **Customer Resolution Team**. Complaints about staff conduct go directly to the **Operations Manager**, not the support desk.

## 12. Things this knowledge base does not cover
International shipping, customs, and fuel surcharges are handled by a separate team and are **not** described in this knowledge base.
